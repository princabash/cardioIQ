Cardiovascular Health Is the Foundation of Healthy Longevity

Living longer is meaningful only if those additional years are lived in good health.

CardioIQ was created with a simple belief: preventing cardiovascular disease is one of the most effective ways to improve both lifespan and healthspan. Scientific evidence consistently shows that cardiovascular health influences not only the risk of heart attack and stroke, but also healthy aging, cognitive function, kidney health, and overall quality of life.

Our mission is not simply to identify cardiovascular risk. It is to help people understand the biological processes that drive that risk and to support earlier, more informed conversations with healthcare professionals.

By combining evidence-based medicine, advanced lipid science, and explainable artificial intelligence, CardioIQ transforms complex laboratory data into meaningful clinical insights that support personalized cardiovascular prevention.

Professional Story

Why CardioIQ Exists

The idea for CardioIQ did not start in a boardroom. It started in the clinic.

Over years of treating patients with dyslipidemia, atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and chronic kidney disease, one pattern repeated itself constantly: patients would arrive holding a stack of lab results, often having already searched online for answers, often more confused than before they searched.

A lipid panel would list LDL, HDL, triglycerides — sometimes ApoB or Lp(a) — but the report itself never explained how these numbers related to one another, what they meant for that specific patient's risk, or what should happen next. Two patients with an identical LDL level could carry very different cardiovascular risk, depending on ApoB, Lp(a), family history, and a dozen other factors a single number on a page cannot capture.

At the same time, the clinical evidence behind these markers was advancing faster than most lab reports could reflect. New risk calculators, new guideline thresholds, new therapies for previously untreatable conditions like elevated Lp(a) — the science kept moving, while the patient experience of receiving a report stayed the same.

CardioIQ was built to close that distance: between what a lab report says and what a patient actually needs to understand; between what current cardiovascular science knows and what most people are told.

Clinical Review Process

How Reports Are Validated

Every CardioIQ report is developed and reviewed against a structured clinical framework, not generated as an unverified AI output.

  • Guideline-anchored logic — Risk interpretation rules are built directly from current ESC/EAS, ACC/AHA, ADA, and KDIGO recommendations.
  • Clinical oversight — Interpretive frameworks and report logic are reviewed by Dr. Gamezardashvili to ensure alignment with current clinical practice standards.
  • Continuous updates — As guidelines are revised and new evidence emerges, CardioIQ's interpretive logic is reviewed and updated accordingly.
  • Source-linked reasoning — Where possible, conclusions reference the specific guideline or evidence basis behind them.
